## 2. Methodology & Formula Transparency

- **Net Annual Benefit:** `Net Benefit = (Verified Labor Savings + Avoided Risk Penalties + Auto-Clear Savings) - (Implementation CapEx + Annual OpEx)`
- **Return on Investment (ROI):** `ROI = (Year 1 Net Benefit / Total Year 1 Investment) * 100`
- **No Double Counting:** Labor savings represent reallocated analyst hours calculated strictly on incremental breaks identified prior to close. Avoided risk penalties reflect late-close adjustments and audit observation remediation fees.
- **3-Year Net Present Value (NPV):** Evaluated at an 8.0% hurdle discount rate.
