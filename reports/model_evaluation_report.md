## 4. Architectural & Governance Recommendation

In accordance with regulatory model governance frameworks (Federal Reserve SR 11-7, OCC Bulletin 2011-12, and SOX 404 controls for financial reporting systems):
1. **Logistic Regression is the Recommended Production Model.** Its PR-AUC and top-10% break capture are within statistical noise of Gradient Boosting, yet it offers complete coefficient auditability, straightforward Excel/SQL portability, and rapid regulator explainability.
2. **Governed Upstream Preventive Action:** Permutation importance demonstrates that `mapping_version_age_days` is by far the #1 driver of break risk. Fixing upstream mapping governance eliminates more breaks than downstream human triage can ever resolve.
